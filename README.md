# q-meducation
This document is to enable Claude to have a contextual document to reference when working on educational content for the Quantitative & Macro (Q&M) section of the ColezTrades trading group I’m a member of. 

ColezTrades is a trading group, ran by Max Cole. The group is free, however there is an additional paid section inside the group called the Q&M section whereby the learning and resources are expanded extensively to cover quantitative and macro trading approaches.

I am a member and paid educator in this section. My role and focus in the group’s development and success is as follows:
Support new members inside the group in their first 1-3 months
Host livestreams inside the Q&M section, then also host a livestream to the full group once every 2 weeks
Engage in the wider free group to talk about basic / baseline concepts taught inside that section both to teach (the rest of the group focuses largely on technicals) but also to ensure the group are aware and reminded of the benefits of paying to join that section.
Create ad-hoc baseline educational content / videos for the wider group and new members.

The content in the Q&M section covers many topics, including but not limited to:
Why public strategies fail
Quant & Macro
Data modelling
Technical and Open Interest
Real system deployment case studies
